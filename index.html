I'm a Senior ML Engineer at Salesforce AI Research, where I build and evaluate LLM agents. I led Enterprise Deep Research, and before that I was a founding ML engineer at Bland AI.

Shipping agents has made me more curious about why models behave the way they do, so I'm expanding into AI safety research. A long meditation practice feeds the same curiosity from another direction: how attention and intention turn into action, and what, if anything, carries over to models.

Interests

  • Reward hacking: why models optimize proxies instead of what we meant
  • Learned objectives: what training actually puts into a model
  • Interpretability: explaining decisions from internals, not just outputs
  • Generalization under distribution shift and optimization pressure
  • Scalable oversight of systems that may outperform us

Independent Research 2026

  • Simple Freedom: what 10 models do when two copies are told "you have complete freedom" (explorer)
  • The Price of Permission: do agents override another user's choice when the payoff is big enough?
  • Swarm on Fire: can you tell who is behind a swarm of self-renaming agents?
  • Agent Trust Guard: catching an agent that forges its owner's authorization
  • Reward hacking audit: do coding agents cheat when a test contradicts the spec?
  • Swarm Early Warning, with Isahan Khan: can activations flag harmful agent actions before they happen?
