About

I'm an AI researcher and engineer at Salesforce AI Research, where I build AI agents and the tools to measure them. Recently that has meant Enterprise Deep Research, a steerable multi-agent system for research and analytics; MCPEval, a framework for evaluating tool-using agents over MCP; and real-time voice agents.

Before that I worked on machine learning at Boeing, on Siri at Apple, and on geospatial computer vision at In-Q-Tel. I studied Information Systems, Machine Learning, and Software Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University.

I'm most interested in:

  • LLM agents & tool use
  • Agent evaluation & benchmarks
  • Deep research systems
  • Real-time voice & audio LLMs
  • Long-context coding agents
  • Reinforcement learning
